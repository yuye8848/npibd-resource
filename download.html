Zenodo record: the evidence matrix is published at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23748327 (CC-BY-4.0) and cited in the manuscript Data Availability section. Gene sets and the scoring function are in the table above and in the GitHub repository.

All files are derived from the published analysis; no new statistics were computed for this site. Program scores use the locked formula (UP mean z minus DOWN mean z for zmean; GSVA ssGSEA for bulk validation).
